\chapter{Verification method}
\label{app:verifica}

Every hourly file is checked against the list of 246 messages before it enters the archive
(\cref{sec:file}). The properties of \crefrange{cap:atmosfera}{cap:suolo} were measured on a
verification sample of 34 validity times: 4 in 2019, 26 in 2024, covering every month, and 4 in
2025. Of these, 16 are at 00 UTC, 16 at 12 UTC, one at 06 UTC and one at 18 UTC.\todo{The 16 validity times at 00 UTC were measured on the sixth hour of the same day's run; the archive now takes them from the thirtieth hour of the previous day's run (issue 72): to be re-measured} Where a number is
measured on a different set, the text says so.

The comparisons with ERA5 use the CHAPTER fields aggregated onto the ERA5 grid with an area-weighted
mean. A ratio between CHAPTER and ERA5 is the ratio of means, that is, the mean of the CHAPTER field
divided by the mean of the ERA5 field over the same population of points, or, where stated, the
ratio of medians.
