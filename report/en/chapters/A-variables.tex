\chapter{The 90 variables}
\label{app:variabili}

\Cref{tab:inventario} lists the 90 variables of an hourly file, read from the messages of one hourly
file; every file of the archive carries the same 246 messages (\cref{sec:file}). shortName, paramId
and units are those returned by eccodes, and the name is that of the ECMWF parameter. The sampling
is instantaneous, accumulated over the hour ending at the validity time (\cref{sec:tempo}), hourly maximum (\cref{cap:raffica})
or static (\cref{sec:statici-orari}). The last column marks the fields with a bitmap of missing
points (\cref{sec:mascheratura}); w.e.\ stands for water equivalent.
