    & reset missed at 17 of the 138 hourly transitions (\SI{12.3}{\percent}), over six days & \ref{sec:10fg-finestra} \\

    & from \SI{71.2}{\percent} (July) to \SI{96.2}{\percent} (January) of the domain, at 12 UTC & \ref{sec:instabilita} \\
  \campo{generatingProcessIdentifier} does not distinguish the accumulated fields
    & 128 in 246 of 246 messages & \ref{sec:tempo} \\
  \campo{al} is a two-season table value per dominant category
    & 8 to 11 values over the domain & \ref{sec:albedo} \\
  \campo{slor} is the slope of the resolved terrain under the ECMWF name of the sub-grid slope; \campo{sdor} is computed on \SI{3}{km} cells
    & \campo{sdor}: ratio of medians \num{0.244} & \ref{sec:orografia} \\
